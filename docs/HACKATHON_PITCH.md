# Hackathon Pitch

## One-line problem

People do not lose money only because a voice is fake; they lose money because an attacker creates authority, fear, urgency, isolation, and a payment decision.

## Solution

SCAMTRACE is an explainable, privacy-preserving conversational attack firewall. It fuses timestamped local speech recognition, scam-language cues, social-engineering tactics, attack progression, and an optional voice signal to intervene before a harmful decision.

## Differentiator

Existing systems can flag unknown callers or potential AI voices. SCAMTRACE owns the intervention-grade connective layer:

**conversation → versioned evidence graph → bounded attack narrative → counter-pressure intervention → privacy-safe case handoff**

A human voice reading a digital-arrest script becomes CRITICAL. A declared AI voice reading a pharmacy reminder remains LOW.

When a coercion chain is detected, SCAMTRACE does not stop at “possible scam.” It names the likely playbook, shows the observed chain, identifies the unsafe action, gives a safe exit, and says what independently verifiable fact could lower concern. That is a security decision architecture, not a dashboard score.

## Technical credibility

- Local whisper.cpp ASR and local language/fusion runtime
- Safe JSON model artifact and no cloud inference
- Explicit tactic taxonomy aligned to documented Indian scam patterns
- Non-linear attack-state engine rather than a keyword-only binary classifier
- Threat Narrative Graph with bounded digital-arrest, bank takeover, courier/customs, family-emergency and remote-device takeover playbooks
- Formal evidence → tactic → playbook graph schema with timestamps, provenance, and no caller-identity claim
- Counter-Pressure Protocol that breaks isolation without ever contacting an alleged caller or authority automatically
- Redacted-by-default incident export plus a user-controlled 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in response handoff
- Actual ablation, decision-contract, adversarial decision, and performance reports, with limitations disclosed

## Judge-proof claim boundary

We do not claim field accuracy, caller identity, criminality or deepfake accuracy. We demonstrate local operation, traceable behaviour evidence, a deterministic intervention policy, model-training provenance, measured development checks, and the concrete research gates required before publication.

## Inclusion

- High contrast, elder mode, plain-language intervention
- Tamil, Hindi, Hinglish-oriented phrase coverage
- Large text and color plus label indicators
- No requirement to send private call audio to a service
